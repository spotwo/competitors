# Authorities

Authorities are first-class sources used to resolve terminology, identifiers, safety requirements, metrics, and industry conventions.

Do not treat all authorities as equivalent:

- **Regulation** - legally applicable only in its jurisdiction.
- **Formal standard / standards body** - normative or consensus reference.
- **Industry association / guide** - strong domain convention, but not necessarily mandatory.
- **Vendor usage** - evidence of market language, not a standard.

A product naming decision should cite the relevant authority records and competitor evidence rather than relying on memory.
